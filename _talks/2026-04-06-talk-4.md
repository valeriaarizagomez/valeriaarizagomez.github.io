This talk was part of the ECPR Joint sessions of workshops 2026 in Innsbruck, in the panel “The rise of toxic and violent communication styles”.

Across Europe, growing political contestation over migration, gender, sexuality, and disability has targeted minoritized groups. These actions are typically associated with far-right and illiberal political parties and media, but we know little about the role of mainstream media in platforming illiberal discourse. This talk examines how German and Polish broadsheet and tabloid newspapers represented migrants, LGBTQ+ people, and disabled people between 2012 and 2024. These two countries have experienced contrasting political trajectories: the electoral consolidation of the Alternative für Deutschland (AfD) in Germany and Poland’s transition from the illiberal rule of the Law and Justice party (PiS) towards political re-liberalization
Bringing together Stuart Hall’s Theory of Representation and Lamont and Molnár’s concept of Symbolic Boundaries, we explore how mainstream news media reproduce, amplify, or challenge processes of othering and social exclusion.
