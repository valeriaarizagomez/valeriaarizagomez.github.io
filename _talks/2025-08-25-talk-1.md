This talk was part of the Panel "Representation at the Crossroads: The Dynamics of Inclusion and Exclusion in Media and Parliamentary Debates" in the ECPR General conference section "Bridging Gaps: Political Communication’s Role in Reducing Democratic Erosion"
The late 2010s and early 2020s a "cultural backlash" against diversity and inclusion took hold of U.S. policy and mainstream media. To challenge the notion that social justice pursuits and inclusion had gone "too far" we focused on key contributors to public disourse: broadsheet news. This talk presented how minoritized groups were represented in the news, and how these representations changed over two decades (2002–2021). This research stage focused on broad categorizations of LGBTQ+ people, migrants, disabled people, and religious minorities, to contrast them to representations of the dominant group (unmarked men) in the following research stage.
